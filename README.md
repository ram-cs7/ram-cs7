## 👨‍🔬 About Me

I am an **AI/ML Engineer and Researcher** working at the intersection of **Large Language Models, Multimodal AI, Machine Learning Systems, and Reliable AI**.

My work spans both research and production-oriented engineering, including **LLM applications, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), AI agents, Vision-Language Models, reinforcement learning, OCR and document intelligence, AI inference infrastructure, backend APIs, and machine learning evaluation**.

My research experience includes **multimodal AI, vision-language models, reinforcement learning, and autonomous driving** using large-scale CARLA simulations. This work resulted in a peer-reviewed publication at the **IEEE/CVF WACV 2026 Workshop**.

On the engineering side, I have built production-oriented AI systems including an **open-source LLM inference gateway** and an OCR pipeline that processed **21,000+ JEE/NEET questions**, reducing manual question-bank creation effort by approximately **80%**.

## 🔬 Research

### Trust-Guided Multimodal LLM Integration with Reinforcement Learning for Autonomous Driving
**IEEE/CVF WACV 2026 Workshop**

Research on trust-aware integration of multimodal LLM guidance into autonomous driving using reinforcement learning and CARLA simulation.

- 2,250 evaluation episodes
- 15 experimental configurations
- 3 random seeds
- Multimodal camera, LiDAR, and telemetry data
- Learned trust-gating mechanism for adaptive LLM utilization

📄 [Paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2026W/LLVM-AD/papers/Chennaka_Trust-Guided_Multimodal_LLM_Integration_with_Reinforcement_Learning_for_Autonomous_Driving_WACVW_2026_paper.pdf)
